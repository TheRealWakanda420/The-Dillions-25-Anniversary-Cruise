      Embarkation / Pre-Cruise Port

      🇹🇷 Istanbul

      Give this city extra time if you can — it sets the tone for the entire anniversary trip.

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • Hagia Sophia — Byzantine-era architectural icon
      • Blue Mosque (Sultan Ahmed Mosque) — hand-painted blue Iznik tiles, six minarets
      • Topkapi Palace — former Ottoman sultan residence, now a museum
      • Basilica Cistern — atmospheric underground Byzantine reservoir
      • Grand Bazaar — one of the world's oldest/largest covered markets (1,200+ shops)
      • Bosphorus Strait cruise — sail the waterway dividing Europe and Asia
      • Galata Tower — panoramic views over Old City and Beyoğlu

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): Tatbak — beloved kebab shop specializing in Gaziantep-style lahmacun since 1960, Nişantaşı district. Fast, reasonably priced, genuinely local.
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Zübeyir Ocakbaşı — charcoal-grill spot in Taksim known for traditional meze and grilled meats; lively, unpretentious, locals return often.
      • $$$ (High-End): Mikla — Michelin-starred, sweeping Bosphorus views. Chef Mehmet Gürs' “New Anatolian Cuisine” tasting menus. Reservations essential.

      Excursion Links

      Compare ship-sponsored and independent options before arrival.

      Port 2

      🇹🇷 Kusadasi (Ephesus)

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • Ephesus — UNESCO World Heritage Site; Library of Celsus, the Great Theater, Fountain of Trajan
      • House of the Virgin Mary — believed final residence of the Virgin Mary
      • St. John's Basilica — 6th-century ruins over the apostle's traditional burial site
      • Temple of Artemis — remains of one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World
      • Şirince Village — hillside village known for fruit wines
      • Kusadasi Marina & Old Bazaar — leather goods, ceramics, local shopping

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): Erzincan Restaurant — family-run since 1997, near the port; exceptional dips, kebabs, homemade baklava.
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Oksa Marina — well-loved seafood spot with fresh mezze, on the water.
      • $$$ (High-End): Tranche — Setur Marina steak and Mediterranean seafood with a dedicated wine program; 4.6/5 rated.

      Excursion Links

      Prioritize Ephesus options early if ancient sites are a must-do for your group.

      NCL
      Port 3

      🇬🇷 Santorini

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • Oia — iconic whitewashed village, world-famous sunset views
      • Fira — island capital, caldera-edge views, cable car to old port
      • Fira-to-Oia caldera hike — ~10km, connects the island's most scenic villages
      • Caldera boat/catamaran cruise — sail past the volcanic islets and hot springs
      • Ancient Akrotiri — excavated Bronze Age Minoan settlement
      • Imerovigli & Skaros Rock — quieter caldera viewpoint
      • Local wineries — Santorini's volcanic-soil wines (Assyrtiko)

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): Ouzeri Lefteris (Karterados) — mezedes and ouzo, working-class local spot, no tourist pricing.
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Metaxy Mas (Exo Gonia) — where locals actually eat; hyper-local ingredients, Greek fusion, hidden gem off the caldera circuit.
      • $$$ (High-End): Selene (Pyrgos) — widely considered the island's most serious restaurant; 30 years of research into Santorinian ingredients, tasting menu.

      Excursion Links

      Choose between caldera scenery, wine tasting, or archaeological tours depending on pace.

      NCL
      Port 4

      🇬🇷 Mykonos

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • Little Venice (Alefkandra) — 18th-century houses perched over the water, best for sunset cocktails
      • Windmills (Kato Mili) — iconic 16th-century windmills above Little Venice
      • Panagia Paraportiani — striking whitewashed church complex, one of Greece's most photographed
      • Mykonos Town (Chora) — whitewashed lanes, boutiques, galleries
      • Delos day trip — UNESCO World Heritage archaeological site, mythical birthplace of Apollo & Artemis
      • Ano Mera village — quieter, centered on the Panagia Tourliani monastery

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): To Maereio (Chora) — beloved taverna run by twin brothers for nearly 20 years; small menu, daily specials, authentic.
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Fokos Taverna — remote north-coast beach taverna, deeply local vibe, seasonal short menu, slow-cooked goat.
      • $$$ (High-End): Krama (Semeli Hotel) — contemporary Greek fine dining, polished courtyard setting, tasting menu.

      Excursion Links

      Mykonos can be scenic or slow-paced; this is a good stop to balance activities with downtime.

      Port 5

      🇬🇷 Athens (Piraeus)

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • The Acropolis & Parthenon — UNESCO World Heritage Site, the essential Athens visit
      • Acropolis Museum — state-of-the-art museum of artifacts from the Acropolis
      • Plaka neighborhood — cobblestone streets at the foot of the Acropolis, neoclassical charm
      • Ancient Agora — well-preserved marketplace and Temple of Hephaestus
      • Monastiraki Square & flea market
      • Rooftop bars with Acropolis views

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): Kosta Souvlaki (Syntagma) — no-frills, fast, authentic souvlaki wraps locals actually queue for.
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Klimataria (Psyrri) — historic taverna since the early 20th century; vine-covered courtyard, iconic slow-cooked lamb.
      • $$$ (High-End): GB Roof Garden — refined cuisine, panoramic Acropolis views, memorable special-occasion dinner.

      Excursion Links

      If this is your must-see history stop, reserve Acropolis-focused tours well in advance.

      NCL
      Port 6

      🇬🇷 Katakolon (Olympia)

      A small, low-key port town and the gateway to Ancient Olympia, birthplace of the Olympic Games.

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • Ancient Olympia — sanctuary and stadium where the ancient Olympic Games were held for centuries
      • Archaeological Museum of Olympia — statues, bronzes, and finds from the site
      • Katakolon waterfront — a compact, walkable strip of cafes and shops right at the port
      • Local olive oil and honey shopping near the port
      • A quick dip at Katakolon Beach for a lower-key day

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): Taverna Antonios — casual, family-run spot near the port for Greek seafood and grilled classics.
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Kourouta beach tavernas — a short drive from port, relaxed waterfront dining favored by locals over the port-front strip.

      Katakolon is a small port town, so options are limited compared to other stops — this is a good day to eat lighter and save the splurge meal for Athens or Corfu.

      Excursion Links

      Book an Olympia tour early in the day if history is the priority; the site is inland and best visited before the midday heat.

      NCL
      Port 7

      🇬🇷 Corfu

      A greener, Venetian-influenced Ionian island, with an old town built for wandering.

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • Corfu Old Town — UNESCO World Heritage Site, Venetian, French, and British architectural layers
      • Old Fortress & Liston promenade — French-style arcade with cafes overlooking the esplanade
      • Achilleion Palace — 19th-century palace built for Empress Elisabeth of Austria
      • Paleokastritsa — dramatic coves, monastery views, and swimming
      • A countryside olive grove or wine tasting outside town

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): Pane e Souvlaki — easy, well-loved Greek street food in Corfu Town.
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Marina's Taverna — slightly off the main tourist streets, unpretentious and consistently recommended by locals; try the grilled octopus.
      • $$$ (High-End): The Venetian Well — Corfu Old Town's most romantic address, tucked into a historic square with a long wine list.

      Excursion Links

      A good stop to balance the old town with a scenic countryside or coastal drive.

      NCL
      Port 8

      🇭🇷 Dubrovnik

      Walk the medieval city walls above the terracotta rooftops of the Old Town.

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • City walls walk — ~2km loop around the Old Town, best done early before the heat and crowds
      • Stradun & Rector's Palace — the Old Town's main limestone-paved street and civic heart
      • Franciscan Monastery — one of the world's oldest working pharmacies
      • Cable car to Mount Srđ for panoramic views over the Old Town and coast
      • Lokrum Island or a short boat trip along the coast

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): Konoba Tabak — a no-frills, locals-only spot known for honest home cooking at real Dubrovnik prices.
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Taj Mahal — a beloved old-town address for Bosnian specialties, despite the name.
      • $$$ (High-End): Restaurant 360 or Proto — special-occasion terrace dining above the harbor with standout seafood.

      Excursion Links

      Book the wall walk for the cooler morning hours if your group wants to beat the crowds.

      NCL
      Port 9

      🇲🇪 Bar, Montenegro

      The ship docks in Bar; most guests use the day to visit the fjord-like Bay of Kotor, about 45 minutes away.

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • Kotor Old Town — UNESCO World Heritage Site, medieval walled city on the bay
      • St. John's Fortress hike — ~1,300+ steps up the city walls for panoramic Bay of Kotor views
      • Perast & Our Lady of the Rocks — artificial islet with a picturesque chapel, near Kotor
      • Bay of Kotor boat tour — fjord-like bay, Blue Cave sea grotto option
      • Stari Bar (Old Bar) — Ottoman-era fortress ruins a short drive from the ship, a quieter alternative to Kotor
      • One of Europe's oldest olive trees, near Stari Bar

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): BBQ Tanjga — Balkan grill, high quality at reasonable prices, favored by locals over tourist-trap alternatives.
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Konoba Scala Santa — best value on the Bay of Kotor without compromising quality; grilled squid, Dalmatian stew.
      • $$$ (High-End): Galion — waterfront, extraordinary bay-and-mountain setting, Mediterranean cuisine; reservations required in summer.

      Excursion Links

      This is a strong port for scenic boat outings if your group wants a lower-impact day.

      Port 10

      🇭🇷 Split

      A lively Dalmatian city built into the palace of a Roman emperor.

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • Diocletian's Palace — a UNESCO World Heritage Roman palace that is now the living city center
      • Peristyle & Cathedral of Saint Domnius — the palace's ceremonial courtyard and bell-tower climb
      • Riva promenade & Green Market — waterfront cafes and the old-town produce market
      • Marjan Hill — walking or hiking trails with views over the city and the Adriatic
      • A short boat trip to a nearby island, if time allows

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): Villa Spiza — tiny, popular, and locally loved, with a short market-led menu that changes daily.
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Konoba Fetivi — traditional Dalmatian seafood near the old town, a longtime local favorite.
      • $$$ (High-End): Bokeria Kitchen & Wine — a stylish, central choice for modern Dalmatian plates and a strong wine list.

      Excursion Links

      The palace and old town are walkable from the port, so this is a good day to explore independently at your own pace.

      Disembarkation / Post-Cruise Port

      🇮🇹 Venice (Ravenna)

      Build in extra time here if possible — this finale deserves a slower, more celebratory pace.

      Highlights & Things to Do

      • St. Mark's Square (Piazza San Marco) — Venice's grand central square
      • St. Mark's Basilica — Byzantine-Gothic-Renaissance church famed for golden mosaics
      • Doge's Palace — former seat of the Venetian Republic's rulers, Gothic masterpiece
      • Grand Canal — best seen by vaporetto (water bus) or gondola
      • Rialto Bridge & Rialto Market — iconic bridge and the city's oldest market
      • Murano & Burano islands — glassblowing island and colorful-house island, worth a half/full day
      • Bridge of Sighs — connects the Doge's Palace to the old prisons

      Restaurants by Price Point

      • $ (Budget/Local): All'Arco (near Rialto Market) — Venice's most famous bacaro, traditional cicchetti (small plates), where locals have eaten for generations. Featured in Stanley Tucci's “Searching for Italy.”
      • $$ (Mid-Range): Vini Da Gigio (Cannaregio) — sophisticated Venetian dishes, canal-side, strong local wine list, seasonal menu sourced from a Venetian-island farmshare.
      • $$$ (High-End): Osteria da Fiore — Michelin-starred, quintessential Venetian canal-side fine dining; a true special-occasion destination.

      Excursion Links

      Use the post-cruise window for a longer city day, island hopping, or a final splurge dinner.
